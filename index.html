The overview

StudyOS reads your learning material (lecture slides, PDFs, notes, code) and sorts it into a tree.

The tree is for the few minutes before you study. It gives you a quick overview of what you're about to learn, so you can see where each idea fits.

It's also a reference for during the session. Keep it open while you study and look back at it whenever you need to, so you never get lost halfway through.

Why start with an overview?

Your brain remembers new ideas better when it already knows where they fit. Decades of studies back this up: students who get a quick overview before a lesson outperform about two-thirds of those who don't.

Sources: Mahar, 1992 · Bransford & Johnson, 1972
